Basic Policies

Masks - Well fitted, high filtration masks (KF94, KN95, or N95 respirators) continue to be required in my practice for anyone 5 and over. Client masks may be removed temporarily to intra-oral work. I am happy to provide you with a new KN95 respirator if you do not have access to any, and I have smaller respirators (KF94 and KN95) available for younger children. If you wish to buy good quality masks for your younger children, options can be found here (KN95), here (KN95 for preschoolers) and here (KF94).

Cancellation - I require 24 hours notice to cancel a massage/bodywork appointment. If you cancel less than 24 hours before appointment there will be a fee of $50. The 24 hours cancellation notice is waived in case of cancellation due to illness.

Illness - I ask that you please reschedule with me if you, your baby, or someone in your household have or have had in the last 10 days a cough, fever, runny nose, sore throat, conjunctivitis, or other symptoms of communicable illness. If you come to an appointment at my home studio, and I learn that you, your baby, or someone in your household is communicably ill or has been communicably ill within the last 10 days, I reserve the right to reschedule the appointment. I also reserve the right to cancel/reschedule an appointment and notify you as soon as possible if I am ill. Thank you for your kind understanding.

Inclement weather, fire, and air quality - If you are unable to safely come to my home studio at the appointed time due to weather, fire, or resultant road conditions/air quality, there is no late cancellation fee. If we are unable to have the windows open for ventilation at my home studio due to poor air quality, I reserve the right to reschedule the appointment and notify you.

Veterinary clearance - In the state of Oregon, you must be able to furnish a referral, prescription, consult, or other clearance from the licensed veterinarian of the animal in your organization’s custody per ORS 686.040 (4) before I can schedule an in-person massage session for the animal.

Gift certificates - Gift certificates are available for purchase, and are a lovely way to provide the growing families in your life with the support and nurturance we all need and deserve. Gift certificates do not expire, have no cash value, and are not redeemable for cash.
