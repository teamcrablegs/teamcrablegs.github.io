Resources

For Pregnancy and Postpartum

Baby Blues Connection - phone, group, and partner support for people experiencing perinatal mood disorders

Postpartum Support International - connect with phone, online, and local perinatal mood disorder support

Brief Encounters - support for bereaved parents

MamaSpace Yoga - wonderful yoga for pregnant and postpartum people that helps make space for babies to assume optimal positions for birth

La Leche League of Beaverton (link leads to facebook) - Meetings online via Zoom every second Thursday at 7:00pm Pacific, and in-person every third Thursday at 10:00am Pacific.

For Dads

Postpartum Support International - Dads’ Chat 1st Monday of each month

The Good Men Project

Basic Training for New Dads - virtual and in-person (San Diego) classes developed by Dr. Daniel Singley

Acupuncture

Jenna Mandzuk LAc. at Living Light Acupuncture (Sherwood)

Whitney Green Acupuncture (Tigard)

For Post-Infection/Chronic Illness

#MEAction - Myalgic encephalomyelitis (ME) support and resources

CFS Self-Help - Information and other resources for people living with ME/CFS and fibromyalgia

American Myalgic Encephalomyelitis and Chronic Fatigue Syndrome Society Financial Crisis Fund - intended to assist patients who find themselves unable to meet a critical need

Body Politic - Long COVID support group

Patient-Led Research Collaborative - Long COVID support group

For Veterans

Veterans’ Crisis Line - dial 988 then press 1 for Veterans; confidential chat also available, click in upper right hand corner of website

Disabled American Veterans - assistance for Veterans in accessing benefits, support, and more

ODVA (Oregon Department of Veterans’ Affairs) - may be able to help with accessing benefits, discharge upgrades, and more

NCTSN’s toolkit Understanding Child Trauma and Resilience for Military Parents and Caregivers

For Pets/Animals

Portland Animal Welfare (PAW) Team - free and low-cost veterinary care for pets of people experiencing homelessness or extreme poverty

Compassionate Care - 24/7 in-home pet euthanasia and cremation service

Dove Lewis Pet Loss Support

Animal Shelter Alliance of Portland (ASAP) resource guide via Oregon Humane Society - find information about medical care, training/behavior, food banks, emergency boarding, safe housing, and wildlife.

Washington County Animal Services - help with lost and found pets, dog licensing, disaster preparedness, resources, and more!
